# Data Processing Agreement Execution Checklist

Use this optional checklist when reviewing a production processor. Store any agreements and
supporting evidence in the approved legal repository, never in Git. It does not gate launch or
provider configuration.

## Processor record

- Processor legal name and service:
- Controller legal entity and authorized signatory:
- Processing purpose and exact product feature:
- Data categories and data-subject categories:
- Planned production start date:
- Register entry and evidence reference:

## Required approvals

- [ ] If desired, DPA/addendum is executed by authorized parties.
- [ ] Instructions, confidentiality, security measures, assistance with data-subject rights,
  deletion/return, audit rights, and breach-notification duties are reviewed.
- [ ] Data residency, international-transfer mechanism, and subprocessors are reviewed.
- [ ] Provider terms confirm the approved training/use and retention posture for this service.
- [ ] If applicable, security/privacy owner and legal/DPO approval are recorded.
- [ ] Privacy policy and subprocessor register have been updated.

## Lifecycle

- Review at least annually and before material provider/model/retention/subprocessor changes.
- On expiry, termination, or a material change, reassess the processor's data handling as needed.
- Record only a non-sensitive contract reference and review date in the subprocessor register.
