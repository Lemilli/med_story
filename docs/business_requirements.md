# MedStory MVP — Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Product Overview

### Product Name

**MedStory**

### Product Vision

MedStory is a personal medical memory application that helps people with chronic or long-term health conditions organize, understand, and preserve their medical history.

The product transforms scattered medical documents, prescriptions, test results, procedures, and personal notes into a structured and searchable medical timeline.

MedStory is **not a diagnostic tool**, **not a replacement for healthcare professionals**, and **not a telemedicine platform**.

Its primary purpose is to help users remember, understand, and communicate their medical history more effectively.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Patients with chronic or long-term medical conditions often face the following challenges:

* Medical records are scattered across multiple sources.
* Test results are difficult to find when needed.
* Users forget important details about previous treatments.
* Medication history becomes difficult to track over time.
* Every new doctor requires patients to repeat their medical story.
* Years of medical history become increasingly difficult to manage.
* Patients struggle to understand complex medical documents and terminology.

As a result, valuable medical information is frequently lost, overlooked, or difficult to communicate.

---

# 3. Product Goal

The goal of MedStory is to provide users with a single place where they can:

* Store medical information.
* Organize medical history automatically.
* Understand medical documents.
* Track significant medical events.
* Review treatment history.
* Prepare concise summaries for healthcare professionals.

---

# 4. Target Audience

## Primary Audience

Individuals living with chronic or long-term health conditions, including but not limited to:

* Chronic pelvic pain
* Chronic prostatitis
* Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)
* Crohn's disease
* Ulcerative colitis
* Chronic neurological conditions
* Autoimmune diseases
* Cancer and long-term oncology care
* Chronic allergies
* Persistent unexplained symptoms

## Secondary Audience

* Parents managing a child's medical history
* Adults managing multiple health conditions
* Individuals who frequently visit healthcare providers
* Patients with large volumes of medical documentation

---

# 5. Core Value Proposition

MedStory helps users answer:

* What symptoms have I experienced over time?
* What treatments have I already tried?
* What medications helped or did not help?
* What tests and procedures have I completed?
* How has my condition evolved over the years?

Instead of maintaining folders, screenshots, and scattered files, users have a continuously updated medical story.

---

# 6. Core Product Principles

## Principle 1 — User-Centered Medical History

The product focuses on the user's health journey rather than individual diagnoses.

Medical diagnoses may change over time.

The user's experience, symptoms, treatments, and medical events remain valuable regardless of diagnostic changes.

---

## Principle 2 — Simplicity

Users should not be required to manually organize complex medical information.

The application should minimize administrative work and reduce data entry whenever possible.

---

## Principle 3 — Long-Term Value

The value of MedStory should increase over time as additional information is added.

The product should become more useful after months and years of usage.

---

## Principle 4 — Clarity

Medical information should be presented in a way that is understandable to non-medical users.

---

# 7. Core User Scenarios

## Scenario A: Adding Medical Information

The user wants to save new medical information.

Examples:

* Test results
* Medical reports
* Prescriptions
* Procedure summaries
* Personal medical notes

### Expected Outcome

The information becomes part of the user's medical history.

---

## Scenario B: Understanding Medical Information

The user wants to understand a medical document.

### Expected Outcome

The application provides a simplified explanation of the document's contents.

---

## Scenario C: Reviewing Treatment History

The user wants to know:

* Which medications were prescribed previously
* Which treatments were attempted
* Which interventions were effective or ineffective

### Expected Outcome

The application presents relevant historical information.

---

## Scenario D: Preparing for a Doctor Visit

The user is visiting a new healthcare provider.

### Expected Outcome

The application generates a concise summary of the user's medical history.

---

## Scenario E: Remembering Past Events

The user wants to review important events in their medical journey.

### Expected Outcome

The application presents a chronological history of symptoms, treatments, examinations, and diagnoses.

---

# 8. Functional Requirements

## Medical History Management

The system shall allow users to maintain a structured medical history.

The history may include:

* Symptoms
* Diagnoses
* Medications
* Examinations
* Procedures
* Hospitalizations
* Treatment outcomes

---

## Document Management

The system shall allow users to store medical documents.

The system shall associate documents with relevant medical information whenever possible.

---

## Medical Timeline

The system shall maintain a chronological timeline of significant medical events.

Users shall be able to review events by date.

---

## Medical Memory

The system shall maintain a continuously updated summary of the user's medical history.

The summary shall represent the user's current understanding of their health journey.

---

## Medical Summary Generation

The system shall generate a concise summary suitable for sharing with healthcare professionals.

The summary should include:

* Key symptoms
* Major diagnoses
* Treatment history
* Important examinations
* Relevant medications

---

## Medical Information Explanation

The system shall help users understand medical terminology and documentation using simplified language.

---

# 9. Non-Functional Business Requirements

## Ease of Use

The application should be usable by non-technical users.

---

## Accessibility

The application should be suitable for users of different ages and levels of technical experience.

---

## Low Data Entry Burden

Users should not be required to manually maintain complex medical records.

Voice-first and AI-assisted interactions should be prioritized whenever possible.

---

## Trust

The application should present itself as a medical organization tool rather than a healthcare provider.

---

## Privacy

Users should feel confident that their medical information remains under their control.

---

# 10. Explicitly Out of Scope for MVP

The MVP will not include:

* Disease diagnosis
* Treatment recommendations
* Prescription generation
* Telemedicine consultations
* Appointment booking
* Direct communication with healthcare providers
* Wearable device integrations
* Insurance integrations
* Hospital integrations
* Specialized AI doctors
* Multi-agent medical systems

---

# 11. Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful if users can:

1. Store their medical history in a single place.
2. Understand their medical information more easily.
3. Review years of treatment history quickly.
4. Prepare for doctor visits with less effort.
5. Reduce the need to repeatedly reconstruct their medical history from memory.

---

# 12. Product Positioning

MedStory is not an AI doctor.

MedStory is not a telemedicine platform.

MedStory is a personal medical memory that helps people with chronic and long-term health conditions organize, understand, and communicate their medical history over time.

---

# One-Sentence Product Definition

> **MedStory is a personal medical memory that automatically transforms years of medical documents, treatments, and health events into a clear, searchable, and understandable health story.**
